Waura privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026. Waura is published by NexBrothers.

Waura turns intentional phone use into an aura score. It is built so that your data never leaves your phone.

The short version

  • Waura has no internet permission. It cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Waura reads only how long each app was open. It never reads messages, photos, or what you watch or post.
  • You can export or delete all of your Waura data at any time.

What Waura uses on your phone

  • App usage time. With your permission (Android's "usage access"), Waura reads how long each app was in the foreground, to score your daily limits. It does not read any content inside apps.
  • The apps you choose. The names and package names of the apps you mark as limited, neutral or essential, and the limits you set.
  • Your focus sessions and scores. Session lengths and times, daily scores, streaks and levels.
  • Your settings. The name you enter, notification preferences, quiet hours and similar choices.

All of this is stored only on your phone, in Waura's private storage. It is not sent to NexBrothers or any third party, because the app cannot connect to the internet.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Waura excludes its data from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. If you move to a new phone, Waura starts fresh there.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Waura shows reminders such as "5 min left" for a limited app, a focus session ending, or a morning recap. They are created on your phone. On the lock screen they show only a generic line, never app names or times.

Sharing

Waura shares nothing on its own. Data leaves your phone only if you choose to: for example, when you use "Export my data" or share a level-up card, Android asks you which app to send it to.

Your choices

  • Turn usage access off in Android settings at any time. Limits then stop counting and never cost aura.
  • Turn any notification type off in Waura's profile or in Android settings.
  • Use "Export my data" in your profile to get a copy, or "Delete all data" to erase everything from your phone. Uninstalling Waura also removes its data.

Children

Waura is made for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
