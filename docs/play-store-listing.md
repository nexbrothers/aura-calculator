# Waura: Google Play listing

Name: **Waura** (W + aura, said "WOW-ra"). Play package ID: `com.nexbrothers.waura`.

## App name (30 characters max)

Waura: Your Screen Time Aura

## Short description (80 characters max)

Build your aura with focus and daily app limits. Everything stays on your phone.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

Your phone time has an aura. Waura turns intentional phone use into an aura score from 0 to 1000, so every focused hour and every limit you keep shows up as a W.

How your aura grows
- Focus sessions: put the phone down for 25, 50 or 90 minutes, or set your own. Each completed minute adds 1 aura, up to 100 a day.
- Daily limits: pick the apps you want to use less and set a daily limit for each. End the day under a limit and that app adds 20 aura at midnight.
- Going over a limit costs 1 aura per minute, up to 60 per app per day.
- Neutral and essential apps, like maps or your bank, never change your aura.
- Ending a focus session early never costs aura.

Five levels to reach
Static, Flicker, Glow, Radiant and Halo. Your aura has its own colours that change as you level up, on the home screen, in the app and in your widgets.

See what changed
- Today, line by line: every focus session and every limit, with what it added or cost.
- The last 7 days and a calendar of your aura by day.
- Streaks for days with a focus session or all limits kept.

Reminders that respect you
- A heads-up when a limited app has 5 minutes left, and when its limit is reached.
- A note when your focus session is done.
- An optional morning recap of yesterday's aura.
- Quiet hours: warnings arrive without sound when you choose.
- The lock screen shows only that something is ready, never which app or how long.

Private by design
- Everything stays on your phone. Waura has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
- No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics.
- Waura reads only how long each app was open. It never sees your messages, photos or what you watch or post.
- Export your data or delete everything from your profile at any time.

Home screen widgets
Keep your aura, your score and your closest limit one glance away.

Waura uses Android's usage access to read how long apps are open. You can turn it off at any time; limits then simply stop counting and never cost aura.

## Play Console answers

- **Category:** Health & fitness (or Productivity).
- **Contains ads:** No.
- **Data safety:** No data collected and no data shared. Usage time is processed only on the device, the app has no internet permission, and the user can delete all data in the app.
- **Target audience:** 16 and over (the app is built for 16 to 26 year olds).
- **Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS):** used only to read how long each app was in the foreground, to score daily limits. Never content.
- **Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), if Play Console asks:** used only so focus sessions and limit warnings arrive on time; the app works without it, a few minutes later.
